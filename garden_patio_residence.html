An estate style residence where soaring ceilings, crystal chandeliers, and curated entertainment spaces redefine what a luxury group stay can be.

The grand main floor opens with coffered crown moulding and custom LED mood lighting throughout. The gourmet kitchen features full height cabinetry and an oversized granite island that flows seamlessly to a formal dining room and an elevated outdoor deck.

Eight elegantly appointed bedrooms occupy the upper level. Five feature private ensuite bathrooms, offering hotel quality privacy. The master suite is a sanctuary, complete with a spa calibre double vanity and a freestanding marble soaker tub.

The fully finished basement is a destination in its own right. A backlit luxury bar with high stools anchors the entertainment lounge, joined by an LED ceiling, electric fireplace, and a games area featuring air hockey and foosball. Outside, a vast estate backyard reveals a stone patio, a signature gazebo, and a private lawn.
