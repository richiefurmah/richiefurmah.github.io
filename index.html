Who We Are

Hospitality with Intention

Elite Host Services delivers tailored corporate housing across the Greater Toronto Area for businesses that value comfort, reliability, and professional standards.

Common Questions

Direct Answers

How do corporate contracts work?+

Most engagements run as six or twelve month corporate contracts that dedicate our residences to your organisation. During the term, your people stay across the homes as your needs require. Monthly arrangements are also available.

How much notice do you need before a stay?+

One week. Tell us who is arriving and when, and the residence is prepared, cleaned, and stocked before they walk in.

What is included in the rate?+

Everything a working stay needs: a fully furnished home, utilities, WiFi, parking, and in suite laundry. Additional services can be arranged around your requirements.

How do invoicing and payment work?+

We issue formal corporate invoices, with payment accepted by credit card, direct deposit, and other channels. Billing terms are set out in your contract.

Is there a formal agreement?+

Yes. Every engagement is covered by a written Corporate Stay Agreement tailored to your contract terms.

Who looks after our people during a stay?+

One owner level point of contact, available 24/7. You will always know who picks up the phone.

What does it cost?+

Rates and terms are tailored to each engagement and depend on duration, residences, and services. Book a call and we will build a quote around your needs.
