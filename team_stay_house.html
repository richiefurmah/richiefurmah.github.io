A brand new contemporary residence where clean, modern finishes and generous shared space set the tone from the moment guests arrive.

Built for groups who value space, the home delivers three distinct lounges across three levels. The main floor opens with a bright open concept living area, a modern white kitchen anchored by a black granite island, a separate dining room beneath globe pendant lighting, and a stylish family room centred around a fireplace and Smart TV.

The upper level features five bedrooms, two kings and three queens, plus a dedicated laundry room and a charming resting lounge with a unique green garden rug. The master suite is complete with a freestanding soaker tub, a double black granite vanity, and a walk in glass shower.

The basement is the entertainment centerpiece. A full size pool table with red felt anchors the games room, joined by a carrom board, a board games collection, and a home theatre projector. A king bedroom suite with private ensuite occupies the lower level for ultimate privacy.
